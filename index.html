How to read this. Prob is the Kalshi Yes mid-price (bid/ask midpoint when the spread is ≤10¢, otherwise last trade) and American is that probability converted to odds. No-vig normalizes every outcome in the event so they sum to 100%, which is the number to hold against a sportsbook's no-vig line. Buy is what it actually costs to buy Yes now: the ask plus Kalshi's estimated taker fee (7% × P × (1−P)), shown as American odds. Moves are in probability points, or % of the old price if you switch Measure. Blue means the price went up (the line shortened); red means it went down (the line drifted).
